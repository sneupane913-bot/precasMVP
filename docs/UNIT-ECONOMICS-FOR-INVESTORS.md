## Why it costs this much

The published price of these AI services is per hour of audio and per million
words. Those numbers look small. Our cost is higher than a quick reading of
them suggests, for five reasons.

**1. We send far more than the student's answer.**

For every single question we send the marker:

| What we send | Roughly |
|---|---|
| The marking instructions | 1,000 words |
| The question and our private marking notes | 100 words |
| The student's last 3 answers, so it can catch contradictions | 300 words |
| The answer itself | 500 words |
| **Total, per question** | **1,900 words** |

The marking instructions alone are sent **17 times per mock**. That is the
single largest cost in the product.

**2. We chose the more expensive transcriber on purpose.**

There is a cheaper, faster version of the same speech model. It costs a third
as much. **We do not use it.** It mishears Nepali-accented English more often,
and mishearing a nervous student is the one mistake this product cannot make.

**3. We pay a second supplier to stand behind the first.**

If the main transcriber fails or is busy, a second one takes over
automatically. It costs about two and a half times more. The student never
notices anything. We absorb it.

**4. Every student is different, and we budget for the expensive one.**

A confident student speaks for 40 seconds and gets it right first time. A
nervous student speaks for the full 60, retries the question, and produces a
longer answer to mark. **These figures are built on the nervous student**,
because that is who this product is for.

**5. A student may retry any question three times.**

Each retry is transcribed and marked again, at full cost. We allow it because
a student who was not heard properly should not be punished for it.

---

## Settings we chose, and why

| Setting | What we chose | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Speech model | Whisper large v3 | The accurate one, not the cheap one |
| Backup transcriber | Deepgram Nova 3 | Runs automatically if the first fails |
| Marking model | Gemini 3.5 Flash Lite | Stable price, no expiry date on it |
| Answer length limit | 400 words | Stops one long answer costing double |
| Feedback length limit | 900 words | Caps the cost of every single reply |
| Retries allowed | 3 per question | Fairness to students we mishear |

The last two matter most. **Both are hard limits.** No single student, however
they behave, can cost us more than the figures above.

---

## The one number

**Two paying students a month covers our entire running cost**, including
hosting and the domain.

Everything after that is profit.
