Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2024

"Your answers belong to you. Nobody at your consultancy can read them, and nobody at PreCAS Practice reads them either unless you ask us to."

1. Information We Collect

We believe in data minimization. We only collect the information absolutely necessary to provide you with the PreCAS Practice service.

Account Information

When you register, we collect your name, email address, and institutional affiliation to create and manage your account securely.

Practice Data

Your practice responses, recordings, and self-evaluations are stored securely. As stated in our plain-english summary, this data is private to you. We do not use it for broad machine learning models without explicit opt-in, nor do we share individual responses with academic institutions.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is utilized strictly to facilitate your interview preparation experience.

  • To authenticate your access to the platform.
  • To provide automated feedback on your practice sessions based solely on the rubric parameters you select.
  • To maintain and improve platform stability and performance through aggregated, anonymized telemetry.
  • To communicate essential service updates or administrative messages.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your practice data with your university, consultancy, or potential employers.

We may share strictly necessary data with trusted third-party service providers (such as secure cloud hosting providers) solely for the purpose of operating our platform. These providers are bound by strict confidentiality agreements and are prohibited from using your data for any other purpose.

4. Your Data Rights

You maintain full control over your personal data on PreCAS Practice.

You have the right to request a complete export of your data at any time. Furthermore, you have the "right to be forgotten." Upon request, we will permanently delete your account and all associated practice data from our active databases and secure backups within 30 days.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@precaspractice.com.
