# Abertay University update — 2 October 2026

Abertay University, Dundee, has been added to the public catalogue. A search for “Abertey” also finds it. The existing 17-question, 30-minute figures describe our full practice sitting, not an official Abertay interview length.

## What the research establishes

Abertay uses CAS Shield and may request a recorded credibility interview about the applicant’s decision to study at Abertay. Its assessment also considers the submitted documents, personal information, financial evidence and questionnaire. The applicant must complete the questionnaire themselves. The university’s checklist says invitations go to the student, and instructions provide preparation/answer timings and one answer per question. Applicants should follow their current invitation.
